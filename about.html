about me

Hi, I'm Camilla.

I'm an AI conversation design specialist with 16 years in design.

At GOL Labs, GOL Airlines' innovation lab, I set the experience direction for GAL — the company's first generative-AI assistant — and lead a portfolio of AI and automation initiatives from discovery to rollout. I design each agent's persona, dialogue, knowledge and guardrails, and I build them hands-on in Copilot Studio and Power Platform.

My path is unusual, and that's the point. Information design for TV news taught me to make the complex clear in seconds. Art direction taught me systems. UX taught me to listen. Voice assistants taught me that a conversation has no screen to hide behind. All of it goes into agents people actually use.

A good week, for me, ends with a problem solved, a user giving good feedback and a healthy team.

      how I work

      Two processes that run side by side

      One shapes what the agent says. The other makes sure it ships safely and keeps improving.

      Conversation design

      1. DiscoverInterviews, shadowing and the real questions people ask.
      2. Persona & voiceWho the agent is, how it speaks and what it never says.
      3. Flows & promptsIntents, dialogue, system prompts and output templates.
      4. Ground & guardCurated knowledge, fallbacks, hand-offs and limits.
      5. Test with usersUsability rounds and QA with the people who will use it.
      6. Measure & iterateAssertiveness, adoption, time saved — then the next version.

      Agent build lifecycle

      1. Maturity & backlogIs the knowledge ready? Prioritise in Azure DevOps or Jira.
      2. Build in DevCopilot Studio, Power Automate, Dataverse and connectors.
      3. EvaluateTest sets, rubrics and evaluator agents.
      4. ReleaseManaged solutions through Dev, Test and Prod pipelines.
      5. MonitorLogs and conversations feed the next improvement.
